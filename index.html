Page 02 · Before you begin
Think you’re a good leader? Prove it.
Don’t choose the answer that makes you look like the best leader.
Choose the one you’d actually pick when nobody is watching.

There are no “perfect” answers here — only honest ones. If you fake it, your result might expose you. 👀
A few things before you begin
• Read each situation carefully before choosing.
• Pick the answer that feels most natural to you.
• There are no right or wrong answers.
• Don't choose what sounds like the “perfect” leader.
• Take your time and trust your first instinct.

Be true to yourself. Be real, be honest, and answer as you would—not as you think a leader is expected to answer.
